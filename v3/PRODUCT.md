# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders and engineers Nat might work with. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a link in a message, usually on a laptop between other tasks, sometimes on a phone. They want to know in under a minute who Nat is and what he has shipped.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Nathaniel Quansah, a software engineer in Accra. The products do the talking: real screenshots of shipped work, each in its own colours. Success is a visitor clicking through to a project or an article, or getting in touch.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, alive. Quietly confident, never salesy. Feels like a well-made Apple product page that a person, not a template, put together.

## Anti-references

- Generic component-library portfolios (shadcn cards, pill navs, badges, gradient blobs).
- Multi-page "case study" sites with diagrams, metrics and CTAs. Nat rejected this as overcomplicated and AI-looking.
- Grey, lifeless minimalism with no colour or imagery.
- Copying given references one-to-one. It is a mood reference: plain words, real product visuals, logos, near-text-only lists.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work. Screenshots carry the page, each project in its own brand colour.
2. Few words. Describe what a project is, not how it was built.
3. Simple structure: home (intro and work), about, writing. Nothing else.
4. Alive through colour, type and small, precise motion, not through more UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus, full keyboard use, and every animation has a reduced-motion alternative. Follows the system light or dark mode.
